Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Your files stay on your device

GB PDF Tools processes your PDF, JPG and Word files directly inside your web browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see or store them.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or documents. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and basic statistics.

Third-party libraries

To work, this site loads open-source code libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, Mammoth and html2pdf.js) from the cdnjs content delivery network. Your browser contacts that network to download them, and it may see your IP address.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads through Google AdSense in the future. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

We may use Google Search Console and similar tools to understand how visitors find our site. These tools report general data and do not include your files.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
