Split PDF

Pick a PDF and type the pages you want to keep, for example 1-3,5. You get a new PDF with only those pages.

How to split a PDF

  1. Tap Choose file and select your PDF.
  2. Type the pages to keep. Use a dash for a range and a comma between parts, for example 2-4,7.
  3. Tap Extract & Download. The result is saved as split.pdf.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded?
No. Everything happens in your browser, so your document stays on your device.

How do I keep just one page?
Type that page number, for example 3.
