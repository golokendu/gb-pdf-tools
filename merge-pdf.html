Merge PDF Files

Combine two or more PDF files into one document. Files are joined in the order you select them.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Tap Choose files and select two or more PDFs.
  2. Tap Merge & Download.
  3. Your combined PDF is saved to your device as merged.pdf.

Tip: to control the order, rename files 1, 2, 3 before selecting them.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded?
No. Merging happens inside your browser, so your documents never leave your device.

Is there a file limit?
There is no fixed limit, but very large files may be slow on older phones.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?
No. Remove the password first, then merge.
