Word to PDF

Convert a Word (.docx) file to PDF. After tapping Convert, choose Save as PDF in the print screen.

How to convert Word to PDF

  1. Tap Choose file and select a .docx file.
  2. Tap Convert to PDF. The print screen opens.
  3. Set the destination to Save as PDF and tap the PDF button.

Text, tables and images are kept. Very complex layouts may look slightly different, so check the result before sharing.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Is my document uploaded?
No. Conversion happens in your browser, so your file stays on your device.

Does it work with old .doc files?
No. Open the file in Word and save it as .docx first.
