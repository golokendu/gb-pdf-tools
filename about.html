About GB PDF Tools

GB PDF Tools is a free website for everyday PDF work. You can merge PDFs, split a PDF into the pages you need, turn JPG and PNG images into a PDF, turn PDF pages into JPG images, and convert simple Word (.docx) files to PDF.

Why we built it

Many online PDF sites ask you to upload your documents to their servers. That can be slow, and it is not ideal for personal papers such as ID copies, invoices or certificates. We wanted a simple tool that is fast, free and respects your privacy.

How it works

All the work happens inside your own browser, on your phone or computer. Your files are not uploaded to us, and we cannot see them. When you close the page, nothing is kept.

What you get

  • Free to use, with no sign-up
  • Works on mobile and desktop
  • No watermark on your results
  • Your files stay on your device

A note on Word to PDF

Word to PDF keeps text, tables and images, but very complex layouts may look slightly different from the original. For documents with special formatting, check the result before sharing it.
